A 200 mm record, full size, for laser cutting or printing.

No track has room for a readable Scene Code, so it is only 1.5 mm tall at the edge. Leave a few steps in a row free on one track (outer tracks need the fewest).

If printing, print at 100% (actual size, not fit to page) and check the 100 mm bar.
